The federal clock
60 days

Federal law gives schools 60 days to finish an initial evaluation after you sign consent — unless your state sets its own clock. Most do. (34 CFR 300.301)

Every year
12 months

The IEP must be reviewed at least once every 12 months — in every state, no exceptions. (34 CFR 300.324)

Every three years
3 years

A reevaluation is due at least every 3 years, unless you and the school agree in writing that one isn't needed. (34 CFR 300.303)

What we don't do

Honest about the edges

This site organises dates and paperwork. It does not give advice about your child's case, and it can't tell you what to say in a meeting.

For that, trained help exists and it is free: every state has a federally funded Parent Training and Information Center. We will point you there every time advice is what you need.
