Glossary · Compensatory Education

Compensatory Education

What it means

Extra services given to make up for services a child should have received but did not — for example, make-up therapy minutes after sessions were missed. It is the standard remedy when an IEP was not delivered as written.

Why it matters

Ask for it by number: 'we are owed 240 minutes of speech' gets further than 'sessions were missed'. A dated log makes the number undeniable.
