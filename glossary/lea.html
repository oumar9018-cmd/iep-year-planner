Glossary · LEA (Local Educational Agency)

LEA (Local Educational Agency)

What it means

The legal name for your school district (or charter school) in special education law — the body responsible for evaluations, IEPs and services. When a rule says 'the LEA must', it means your district must.

Why it matters

Letters and complaints are addressed to the LEA — knowing the name helps you write to the office that actually carries the duty.
