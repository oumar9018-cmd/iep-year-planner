IEP evaluation timelines by state

Federal law gives schools 60 calendar days to finish an initial IEP evaluation — unless a state set its own timeframe, which most did. Here is every state's own clock, from its official published rules: how long, how the days count, and when the clock starts.

Fastest calendar-day clock

Alabama — 60 calendar days

Among the 30 states that count calendar days.

Fastest school-day clock

South Dakota — 25 school days

Among the 16 states that count school days. School-day and calendar-day clocks are different measures — a school-day deadline usually lands later on the calendar.
