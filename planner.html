How the dates are worked out

  • Annual review: 12 months after the last one — federal law requires a review at least once a year.
  • Reevaluation: 3 years after the last evaluation — the federal outside date, unless you and the school have agreed in writing that one is not needed.
  • Initial evaluation: your state's own timeline, taken from its published rules (the source is linked on your state's page). States that count school days are counted here Monday to Friday — an approximation: school holidays and breaks are not counted here, and your district's calendar decides the exact date.
  • Transition planning: it must be in the IEP that is in effect on the birthday shown — your state's age, or 16 where the state follows the federal rule.

These dates are planning aids based on published rules, not legal advice. If a date here disagrees with a notice from your school, the school's notice and your Parent Center are the place to check it.
