IEP timelines in Wyoming

The dates that govern your child's IEP in Wyoming, taken from the state's published rules. The official source for each figure is linked at the bottom of this page.

We are still double-checking one detail of this state's figures against a second official source. The source used is linked below, and your Parent Center can confirm the figures.
Initial evaluation60 days
The clock starts on the date the parent signs consent for the evaluation.
Annual IEP reviewAt least once every 12 months
Federal rule — it applies in every state, including this one.
ReevaluationAt least once every 3 years, unless you and the school agree in writing that it is not needed.
Federal rule — it applies in every state, including this one.
Transition planningFrom age 16 — it must be in the IEP in effect on that birthday.
WDE's 2025 verification monitoring report applies the federal standard (34 CFR 300.320(b)): transition services beginning not later than the first IEP to be in effect when the child turns 16. No younger Wyoming-specific transition age was found in state sources.

Wyoming's Chapter 7 rules incorporate 34 CFR 300.301 by reference and set no separate state timeframe; WDE states the timeline simply as '60 days' with no calendar/school qualifier. The incorporated federal text runs from receipt of parental consent and carries the §300.301(d) exceptions (parent repeatedly fails or refuses to produce the child; student enrolls in another LEA after the timeframe began).
