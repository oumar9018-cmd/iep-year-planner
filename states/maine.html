IEP timeline in Maine

The short answer

How many days does a school in Maine have to finish an initial IEP evaluation?

45 school days

The clock starts on the date you sign consent for the evaluation.

Annual IEP review
12 months

At least once every 12 months. This is the federal rule (34 CFR 300.324) — it applies in every state, including Maine.

Reevaluation
3 years

At least once every 3 years, unless you and the school agree in writing that one is not needed. (Federal rule, 34 CFR 300.303.)

Transition planning
Age 16

Maine rule (MUSER IX.3.A(1)(h)) keys the post-secondary transition plan to grade rather than age alone: it must be completed not later than 9th grade or age 16, whichever comes first, and updated annually. Age recorded as 16 per the official Maine DHHS guide 'High School & Beyond: A Guide to Transition Services in Maine' (maine.gov): youth 'will receive transition services no later than ninth grade or at age 16, whichever happens first'.

The details

Maine's evaluation clock, in the state's own words

Maine DOE timeline information sheet (Jan. 22, 2026): public-school initial evaluations must be completed within 45 school days of receiving parental consent; Child Development Services (CDS, preschool) initial evaluations within 60 calendar days. The consent form must be sent within 15 school days of receipt of referral, and the eligibility meeting falls within the 45-school-day timeframe.
