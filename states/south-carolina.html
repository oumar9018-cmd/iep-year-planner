State timelines · South Carolina

IEP timeline in South Carolina

The short answer

How many days does a school in South Carolina have to finish an initial IEP evaluation?

60 calendar days

The clock starts on the date you sign consent for the evaluation.

Annual IEP review
12 months

At least once every 12 months. This is the federal rule (34 CFR 300.324) — it applies in every state, including South Carolina.

Reevaluation
3 years

At least once every 3 years, unless you and the school agree in writing that one is not needed. (Federal rule, 34 CFR 300.303.)

Transition planning
Age 13

SBE Regulation 43-243(III)(G), verified directly in the official SC Code of Regulations (Chapter 43): 'Beginning not later than the first IEP to be in effect when the child turns 13 years, or younger if deemed appropriate by the IEP team, and updated annually thereafter,' the IEP must include measurable postsecondary goals and transition services. Age 13 is the earliest transition age in this chunk and earlier than the federal floor of 16.

The details

South Carolina's evaluation clock, in the state's own words

Stated in an SCDE due-process hearing decision applying SBE Regulation 43-243 and SCDE's SEED procedures document: the timeframe for conducting an initial evaluation is 60 days (calendar days) from receipt of informed parental consent; the decision also records the follow-on steps of 15 calendar days to determine eligibility and 30 calendar days to develop/implement the IEP.

Last verified: 14 November 2026 — checked against the official source named below.
