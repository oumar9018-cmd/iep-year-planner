IEP timelines in Missouri

The dates that govern your child's IEP in Missouri, taken from the state's published rules. The official source for each figure is linked at the bottom of this page.

Initial evaluation60 calendar days
The clock starts on the date the parent signs consent for the evaluation.
Annual IEP reviewAt least once every 12 months
Confirmed in this state's own published rules.
ReevaluationAt least once every 3 years, unless you and the school agree in writing that it is not needed.
Confirmed in this state's own published rules.
Transition planningFrom age 16 — it must be in the IEP in effect on that birthday.
Currently 16: DESE's November 2024 compliance guidance states postsecondary transition planning 'is to be in place by the time the child reaches age 16.' UPCOMING CHANGE: DESE has announced a Missouri State Plan change effective the 2027-28 school year requiring postsecondary transition services in the IEP before the student exits eighth grade (younger if the IEP team determines appropriate).

Part of Missouri's 30-60-30 sequence under the State Plan for Special Education: Review of Existing Data within 30 calendar days of referral, evaluation completed within 60 calendar days of consent, initial IEP within 30 calendar days of the eligibility determination. Per the DESE Parent's Guide, timelines may be extended for acceptable reasons such as snow days, child illness, or summer breaks; DESE compliance guidance adds that weekends can extend a timeline only during winter or summer break, and 60-day timelines are counted as five-day weeks even in four-day-week districts.
