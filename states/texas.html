IEP timelines in Texas

The dates that govern your child's IEP in Texas, taken from the state's published rules. The official source for each figure is linked at the bottom of this page.

Initial evaluation45 school days
The clock starts on the date the parent signs consent for the evaluation.
Annual IEP reviewAt least once every 12 months
Federal rule — it applies in every state, including this one.
ReevaluationAt least once every 3 years, unless you and the school agree in writing that it is not needed.
Confirmed in this state's own published rules.
Transition planningFrom age 14 — it must be in the IEP in effect on that birthday.
TEA Secondary Transition Guidance: under Texas law (TEC §29.0111; 19 TAC §89.1055), transition planning must begin not later than when the student reaches 14 years of age, and the first IEP in effect when the student turns 14 must include measurable postsecondary goals and transition services.

19 TAC §89.1011 / TEC §29.004. If the student is absent 3 or more days during the timeline, it is extended by the number of days absent. If consent is received at least 35 but fewer than 45 school days before the last instructional day, the written evaluation report must be provided to the parent not later than June 30. 'School day' includes shortened instructional days (§89.1011(g)). The eligibility ARD follows within 30 calendar days of the completed report.
