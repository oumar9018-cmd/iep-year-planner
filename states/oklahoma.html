The dates that govern your child's IEP in Oklahoma, taken from the state's published rules. The official source for each figure is linked at the bottom of this page.

Checking note: Oklahoma's education agency currently gives the transition-planning start as the beginning of 9th grade or age 15 on its website, while its handbook and policies documents say 16. We show 15, from the current webpage.

Transition planningFrom age 15 — it must be in the IEP in effect on that birthday.
CONFLICT WITHIN OSDE SOURCES: Oklahoma's rule is grade-plus-age based, not a single age. OSDE's current Secondary Transition webpage (oklahoma.gov) states transition services must be addressed and in effect 'not later than the beginning of the student's ninth grade year or upon turning 15 years of age, whichever comes first' (plan developed during 8th grade, typically age 14). However, OSDE's Secondary Transition Handbook (revised October 2023) and OSDE Special Education Policies state the same trigger as 'beginning of the ninth grade year or upon turning 16 years of age, whichever comes first.' Recorded 15 from the current OSDE webpage; the binding practical trigger is the start of 9th grade.

OSDE procedural safeguards (amended September 2024): initial evaluation conducted within a 45-school-day timeframe running from the date of parental written consent until the initial eligibility determination. The timeframe does not apply if the parent repeatedly fails or refuses to produce the child for the evaluation.
