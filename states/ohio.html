State timelines · Ohio

IEP timeline in Ohio

The short answer

How many days does a school in Ohio have to finish an initial IEP evaluation?

60 calendar days

The clock starts on the date you sign consent for the evaluation.

Annual IEP review
12 months

At least once every 12 months. This is the federal rule (34 CFR 300.324) — it applies in every state, including Ohio.

Reevaluation
3 years

At least once every 3 years, unless you and the school agree in writing that one is not needed — confirmed in Ohio's own published rules.

Transition planning
Age 14

OAC 3301-51-07(E)(2), verified directly in the rule text: transition services beginning not later than the first IEP to be in effect when the child turns fourteen, or younger if determined appropriate by the IEP team, and updated annually thereafter.

The details

Ohio's evaluation clock, in the state's own words

OAC 3301-51-06(B)(4)(a): initial evaluation must be conducted within sixty days of receiving parental consent; 3301-51-06(A)(4) frames this as the 'sixty calendar day time frame.' Exceptions: parent repeatedly fails or refuses to produce the child; child transfers to a new school district of residence (receiving district must show sufficient progress and agree with the parent on a completion date).
