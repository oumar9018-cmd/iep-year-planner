IEP timeline in Mississippi

The short answer

How many days does a school in Mississippi have to finish an initial IEP evaluation?

60 calendar days

The clock starts on the date you sign consent for the evaluation.

Annual IEP review
12 months

At least once every 12 months — Mississippi publishes the same rule in its own guidance.

Reevaluation
3 years

At least once every 3 years, unless you and the school agree in writing that one is not needed — confirmed in Mississippi's own published rules.

Transition planning
Age 14

Same Rule 74.19 document, section 300.320(b): beginning not later than the first IEP to be in effect when the child turns fourteen (14), or younger if determined appropriate by the IEP committee, the IEP must include measurable postsecondary goals and transition services, updated annually thereafter. MDE's Indicator 13 materials likewise say Mississippi begins postsecondary planning at age 14.

The details

Mississippi's evaluation clock, in the state's own words

State Board Policy ch. 74, Rule 74.19, section 300.301(c)(1): initial evaluation must be conducted within 60 calendar days of receiving parental consent. Exceptions (300.301(d)-(e)): parent repeatedly fails or refuses to produce the child, or the child transfers to another public agency mid-timeline and the new agency is making sufficient progress with an agreed specific completion date. Eligibility must then be determined within 14 calendar days after completion of assessments (300.306(a)).
