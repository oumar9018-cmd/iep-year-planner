IEP timelines in California

The dates that govern your child's IEP in California, taken from the state's published rules. The official source for each figure is linked at the bottom of this page.

Initial evaluation60 calendar days
The clock starts on the date the parent signs consent for the evaluation.
Annual IEP reviewAt least once every 12 months
Confirmed in this state's own published rules.
ReevaluationAt least once every 3 years, unless you and the school agree in writing that it is not needed.
Confirmed in this state's own published rules.
Transition planningFrom age 16 — it must be in the IEP in effect on that birthday.
56043(g)(1): if determined appropriate by the IEP team, transition goals/services begin when the pupil starts their high school experience, and in any case 'not later than the first individualized education program to be in effect when the pupil is 16 years of age or younger, as appropriate'. Age 16 remains the mandatory ceiling.

Ed. Code 56043(f)(1): IEP developed within a total time not to exceed 60 calendar days, NOT counting days between the pupil's regular school sessions or terms, or days of school vacation in excess of five schooldays, from receipt of written consent; parent/guardian may agree in writing to an extension (56344). 56043(c) states the assessment determinations + IEP meeting occur within 60 days of receiving parental consent.
