IEP timelines in New York

The dates that govern your child's IEP in New York, taken from the state's published rules. The official source for each figure is linked at the bottom of this page.

Initial evaluation60 calendar days
The clock starts on the date the parent signs consent for the evaluation.
Annual IEP reviewAt least once every 12 months
Confirmed in this state's own published rules.
ReevaluationAt least once every 3 years, unless you and the school agree in writing that it is not needed.
Confirmed in this state's own published rules.
Transition planningFrom age 15 — it must be in the IEP in effect on that birthday.
8 NYCRR 200.4(d)(2)(ix)(a): transition planning beginning not later than the first IEP to be in effect when the student is age 15 (and at a younger age if determined appropriate), updated annually. New York is earlier than the federal floor of 16 but later than the age-14 states.

8 NYCRR 200.4(b)(7) states the initial evaluation must be completed within 60 days of receiving parental consent; NYSED guidance/interpretation treats these as calendar days. Exceptions: student transfers to another district mid-timeline; parent repeatedly fails or refuses to produce the student. Note: a separate 60 SCHOOL days clock in 200.4(d)-(e) applies to arranging programs/services after consent for services, not to the evaluation itself.
