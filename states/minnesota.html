IEP timelines in Minnesota

The dates that govern your child's IEP in Minnesota, taken from the state's published rules. The official source for each figure is linked at the bottom of this page.

Initial evaluation30 school days
The clock starts on the date the parent signs consent for the evaluation.
Annual IEP reviewAt least once every 12 months
Confirmed in this state's own published rules.
ReevaluationAt least once every 3 years, unless you and the school agree in writing that it is not needed.
Confirmed in this state's own published rules.
Transition planningFrom age 14 — it must be in the IEP in effect on that birthday.
Minn. R. 3525.2810, subp. 1A(7): the IEP must include, 'beginning at age 14, and updated annually,' a statement of the pupil's transition service needs. Related provisions frame the same duty by grade: Minn. R. 3525.2900, subp. 4 and Minn. Stat. 125A.08(b)(1) require the IEP to address transition needs during grade 9.

Minnesota Rules 3525.2550, subp. 2: evaluation within a reasonable time not to exceed 30 school days from the date the district receives parental permission, unless a conciliation conference or due process hearing is requested.
