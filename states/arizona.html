IEP timelines in Arizona

The dates that govern your child's IEP in Arizona, taken from the state's published rules. The official source for each figure is linked at the bottom of this page.

Initial evaluation60 days (statute); calendar days per Arizona Dept. of Education guidance
The clock starts on the date the parent signs consent for the evaluation.
Annual IEP reviewAt least once every 12 months
Confirmed in this state's own published rules.
ReevaluationAt least once every 3 years, unless you and the school agree in writing that it is not needed.
Confirmed in this state's own published rules.
Transition planningFrom age 16 — it must be in the IEP in effect on that birthday.
ADE AZ-TAS FAQ (revised Feb 2026, reflecting Oct 2024 State Board rule change): 'In Arizona, transition services are to be in place when a student ends 9th grade or turns 16 years old, whichever is first, or earlier, as determined necessary by the student's IEP team.' Age 16 is the age ceiling; the 9th-grade trigger can start it earlier.

ARS 15-766(B) says 'within sixty days' without a day-type qualifier. ADE's official Timelines FAQ (azed.gov) states: 'The 60-day timeframe for an evaluation or reevaluation refers to 60 calendar days and would include school breaks', and AAC R7-2-401(E)(5) allows a +30-day extension by written parent-school agreement in the child's best interest.
