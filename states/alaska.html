IEP timelines in Alaska

The dates that govern your child's IEP in Alaska, taken from the state's published rules. The official source for each figure is linked at the bottom of this page.

We are still double-checking one detail of this state's figures against a second official source. The source used is linked below, and your Parent Center can confirm the figures.
Initial evaluation90 calendar days
The clock starts on the date the parent signs consent for the evaluation.
Annual IEP reviewAt least once every 12 months
Confirmed in this state's own published rules.
ReevaluationAt least once every 3 years, unless you and the school agree in writing that it is not needed.
Confirmed in this state's own published rules.
Transition planningFrom age 16 — it must be in the IEP in effect on that birthday.
AS 14.30.278(b) frames transition services for a child 'over 15 years of age'; 4 AAC 52.140 adopts 34 CFR 300.320-300.328 (first IEP in effect at 16) by reference, and the State of Alaska GCDSE Transition Handbook states a postsecondary transition plan is required when students reach 16. No Alaska-specific age below 16 was found.

4 AAC 52.115(a) is broader than evaluation alone: within 90 calendar days after obtaining parental consent the district shall evaluate the child, develop an IEP if eligible and consent for services is obtained, and provide services. No break/summer exclusions stated in the rule text.
