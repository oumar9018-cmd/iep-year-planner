IEP timelines in Tennessee

The dates that govern your child's IEP in Tennessee, taken from the state's published rules. The official source for each figure is linked at the bottom of this page.

Initial evaluation60 calendar days
The clock starts on the date the parent signs consent for the evaluation.
Annual IEP reviewAt least once every 12 months
Confirmed in this state's own published rules.
ReevaluationAt least once every 3 years, unless you and the school agree in writing that it is not needed.
Confirmed in this state's own published rules.
Transition planningFrom age 14 — it must be in the IEP in effect on that birthday.
TDOE family guide to the transition-focused IEP (September 2025): Tennessee requires transition planning to begin at age 14 under State Board of Education Rule 0520-01-09-.12 — earlier than the federal age 16.

The timeline covers both the initial evaluation and the eligibility determination, counted from receipt of parental consent for the initial evaluation; cited to State Board of Education Rule 0520-01-09-.05(1)(f) and 34 CFR 300.301.
