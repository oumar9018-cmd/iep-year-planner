IEP timelines in Kentucky

The dates that govern your child's IEP in Kentucky, taken from the state's published rules. The official source for each figure is linked at the bottom of this page.

Initial evaluation60 school days
The clock starts on the date the parent signs consent for the evaluation.
Annual IEP reviewAt least once every 12 months
Confirmed in this state's own published rules.
ReevaluationAt least once every 3 years, unless you and the school agree in writing that it is not needed.
Confirmed in this state's own published rules.
Transition planningFrom age 14 — it must be in the IEP in effect on that birthday.
707 KAR 1:320 Section 7(1): in the child's eighth-grade year or when the child reaches age 14 (or earlier if the ARC determines appropriate), the IEP must include a statement of transition service needs, updated annually; Section 7(2) requires measurable postsecondary goals and transition services by the 16th birthday.

707 KAR 1:320 Section 2(3)-(5): within the 60 school days the child must be evaluated and, if eligible, services provided per the IEP; the ARC must meet to develop the IEP within 30 days of the eligibility determination. Exceptions: child moves to a new LEA (sufficient progress plus parent/LEA agreement on a specific time) or parent repeatedly fails or refuses to produce the child.
