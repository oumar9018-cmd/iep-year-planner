IEP timelines in Nevada

The dates that govern your child's IEP in Nevada, taken from the state's published rules. The official source for each figure is linked at the bottom of this page.

Initial evaluation45 school days
The clock starts on the date the parent signs consent for the evaluation.
Annual IEP reviewAt least once every 12 months
Confirmed in this state's own published rules.
ReevaluationAt least once every 3 years, unless you and the school agree in writing that it is not needed.
Confirmed in this state's own published rules.
Transition planningFrom age 14 — it must be in the IEP in effect on that birthday.
Adopted Regulation R058-24 amended NAC 388.133 and 388.284 to unify transition requirements at age 14: for a pupil who is 14 or older, or who will attain 14 during the IEP period, transition services must include both the courses of study AND postsecondary goals/services. Previously, courses of study began at 14 but the postsecondary-goal elements began at 16.

NAC 388.337(1)(a): initial evaluation within 45 school days after the parent provides informed written consent. The Superintendent of Public Instruction may extend the deadline by not more than 15 school days on the public agency's request (388.337(2)). The deadline does not apply if the pupil transfers in mid-timeline and the new agency is making sufficient progress with a parent-agreed completion date, or if the parent repeatedly fails or refuses to deliver the pupil (388.337(3)). 'School day' is defined in 388.337(4) as a day pupils are scheduled for instruction/testing.
