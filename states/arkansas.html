State timelines · Arkansas

IEP timeline in Arkansas

The short answer

How many days does a school in Arkansas have to finish an initial IEP evaluation?

60 calendar days

The clock starts on the date you sign consent for the evaluation.

Annual IEP review
12 months

At least once every 12 months — Arkansas publishes the same rule in its own guidance.

Reevaluation
3 years

At least once every 3 years, unless you and the school agree in writing that one is not needed — confirmed in Arkansas's own published rules.

Transition planning
Age 16

6 CAR 130-808(b): 'Beginning not later than the first IEP to be in effect when the child turns sixteen (16), or younger, if determined appropriate by the IEP team and updated annually thereafter.' Also codified in Ark. Code 6-41-217(b)(3)(H).

The details

Arkansas's evaluation clock, in the state's own words

6 CAR 130-603(a). Exceptions (130-603(c)): parent repeatedly fails or refuses to produce the child; or child enrolls in another public agency mid-timeline (applies only if new agency is making sufficient progress and parent/agency agree a completion date). A separate evaluation/programming conference must follow within 30 calendar days (130-606).
