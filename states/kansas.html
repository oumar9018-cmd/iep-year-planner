State timelines · Kansas

IEP timeline in Kansas

The short answer

How many days does a school in Kansas have to finish an initial IEP evaluation?

60 school days

The clock starts on the date you sign consent for the evaluation.

Annual IEP review
12 months

At least once every 12 months — Kansas publishes the same rule in its own guidance.

Reevaluation
3 years

At least once every 3 years, unless you and the school agree in writing that one is not needed — confirmed in Kansas's own published rules.

Transition planning
Age 14

KSDE parent guide 'Transition in the IEP' (May 2026): state regulations require districts to begin transition planning no later than the student's 14th birthday, or younger when appropriate; postsecondary goals become part of the IEP the year the student turns 14. Matches K.S.A. 72-3429(c)(8) ('beginning at age 14, and updated annually').

The details

Kansas's evaluation clock, in the state's own words

KSDE Process Handbook Ch. 2: the 60-school-day timeline begins when the school receives written parental consent for the initial evaluation. Under K.A.R. 91-40-8(f)-(g) the evaluation, eligibility meeting and IEP implementation must be completed within 60 school days of receipt of written consent, unless the agency obtained written parental consent to an extension; standard federal exceptions (parent fails/refuses to produce child; transfer) also apply.
