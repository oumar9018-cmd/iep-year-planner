State timelines · Pennsylvania

IEP timeline in Pennsylvania

The short answer

How many days does a school in Pennsylvania have to finish an initial IEP evaluation?

60 calendar days

The clock starts on the date you sign consent for the evaluation.

Annual IEP review
12 months

At least once every 12 months. This is the federal rule (34 CFR 300.324) — it applies in every state, including Pennsylvania.

Reevaluation
3 years

At least once every 3 years, unless you and the school agree in writing that one is not needed. (Federal rule, 34 CFR 300.303.)

Transition planning
Age 14

Same Chapter 14 source, 14.131(a)(5): 'For students who are 14 years of age or older, a transition plan that includes appropriate measurable postsecondary goals related to training, education, employment and, when appropriate, independent living skills.' Confirms the historical hint that Pennsylvania requires transition planning from age 14.

The details

Pennsylvania's evaluation clock, in the state's own words

22 Pa. Code 14.123(b): initial evaluation completed and evaluation report presented to parents no later than 60 calendar days after the agency receives written parental consent, EXCEPT that the calendar days from the day after the last day of the spring school term up to and including the day before the first day of the subsequent fall school term are NOT counted (summer break excluded from the clock).
