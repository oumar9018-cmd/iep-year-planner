IEP timelines in Washington

The dates that govern your child's IEP in Washington, taken from the state's published rules. The official source for each figure is linked at the bottom of this page.

Initial evaluation40 school days
The clock starts on the date the parent signs consent for the evaluation.
Annual IEP reviewAt least once every 12 months
Confirmed in this state's own published rules.
ReevaluationAt least once every 3 years, unless you and the school agree in writing that it is not needed.
Confirmed in this state's own published rules.
Transition planningFrom age 16 — it must be in the IEP in effect on that birthday.
WAC 392-172A-03090(1)(k): beginning not later than the first IEP to be in effect when the student turns 16 (or younger if the IEP team determines it appropriate), the IEP must include measurable postsecondary goals and transition services — matches the federal floor.

WAC 392-172A-03005(3), as amended by WSR 26-21-034 effective November 14, 2026: the district must make a completed draft copy of the evaluation report available to the parent within 35 school days and convene a meeting to arrive at the eligibility decision within 40 school days after the date written consent is provided. Exceptions: the parent repeatedly fails or refuses to produce the child; the student enrolls in another district after consent; the timeline may be extended by documented parent agreement. Before this amendment the rule set a single 35-school-day deadline for the evaluation.
