State timelines · Vermont

IEP timeline in Vermont

The short answer

How many days does a school in Vermont have to finish an initial IEP evaluation?

60 calendar days

The clock starts on the date you sign consent for the evaluation.

Annual IEP review
12 months

At least once every 12 months. This is the federal rule (34 CFR 300.324) — it applies in every state, including Vermont.

Reevaluation
3 years

At least once every 3 years, unless you and the school agree in writing that one is not needed — confirmed in Vermont's own published rules.

Transition planning
Age 16

Vermont follows the federal age: AOE Indicator 13 guidance is written for IEPs of students ages 16 through 21 and requires appropriate measurable postsecondary goals for youth aged 16 and above.

The details

Vermont's evaluation clock, in the state's own words

Rule 2362.2.1(c): the 60 days run from the date parental consent is received by the LEA or, where the evaluation rests solely on review of existing data, from the date on the LEA's notice. If the evaluation will exceed 60 calendar days, the LEA must inform the parent in writing before the end of the timeline (AOE Evaluation Implementation Guide §5.5).
