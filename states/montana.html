State timelines · Montana

IEP timeline in Montana

The short answer

How many days does a school in Montana have to finish an initial IEP evaluation?

60 calendar days

The clock starts on the date you sign consent for the evaluation.

Annual IEP review
12 months

At least once every 12 months. This is the federal rule (34 CFR 300.324) — it applies in every state, including Montana.

Reevaluation
3 years

At least once every 3 years, unless you and the school agree in writing that one is not needed — confirmed in Montana's own published rules.

Transition planning
Age 16

OPI's official Transition IEP form (Jan. 2026): 'TRANSITION SERVICES - For ALL students beginning with the IEP to be in effect when the child is 16 and updated annually thereafter.' Montana follows the federal age-16 standard.

The details

Montana's evaluation clock, in the state's own words

Montana OPI guide (Q16): all assessments on the Evaluation Plan must be completed within 60 calendar days of receiving parental consent for an initial evaluation. The timeline does not apply if the parent repeatedly fails or refuses to produce the student for the evaluation, and special rules apply when a student transfers to another district mid-evaluation.
