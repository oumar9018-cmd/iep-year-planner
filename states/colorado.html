IEP timelines in Colorado

The dates that govern your child's IEP in Colorado, taken from the state's published rules. The official source for each figure is linked at the bottom of this page.

Initial evaluation60 calendar days
The clock starts on the date the parent signs consent for the evaluation.
Annual IEP reviewAt least once every 12 months
Confirmed in this state's own published rules.
ReevaluationAt least once every 3 years, unless you and the school agree in writing that it is not needed.
Confirmed in this state's own published rules.
Transition planningFrom age 15 — it must be in the IEP in effect on that birthday.
Colorado ECEA requires transition services 'to begin with the first IEP developed when the child is age 15, but no later than the end of 9th grade, or earlier if deemed appropriate by the IEP team' (ECEA 4.03(6)(d)) - one year earlier than the federal age-16 floor.

ECEA Rule 4.02(3)(c). CDE IEP FAQ 1(a): the date of receipt of the parent's written consent triggers the timeline; the corresponding ECEA rule requires completion within 60 calendar days from that point. No break/summer exclusion stated in the CDE FAQ text.
