IEP timelines in Georgia

The dates that govern your child's IEP in Georgia, taken from the state's published rules. The official source for each figure is linked at the bottom of this page.

Initial evaluation60 calendar days
The clock starts on the date the parent signs consent for the evaluation.
Annual IEP reviewAt least once every 12 months
Confirmed in this state's own published rules.
ReevaluationAt least once every 3 years, unless you and the school agree in writing that it is not needed.
Confirmed in this state's own published rules.
Transition planningFrom age 16 — it must be in the IEP in effect on that birthday.
Rule 160-4-7-.06(2): transition services beginning 'not later than entry into ninth grade or by age 16, whichever comes first, or younger if determined appropriate by the IEP Team'. GaDOE GO-IEP help (sped.gadoe.org): transition plan 'required for students over the age of 16 or upon entry to 9th grade or above, whichever comes first'. Age 16 is the age ceiling; 9th-grade entry can trigger it earlier.

Rule 160-4-7-.04(1)(b): holiday periods and other circumstances when children are not in attendance for five consecutive school days are NOT counted (including contiguous weekend days), and any summer vacation period when the majority of the LEA's teachers are not under contract is not counted - EXCEPT consent received 30+ days before the end of the school year must still be completed within the 60 calendar day timeframe.
